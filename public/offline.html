Offline mode

You are offline. Live aviation data is unavailable.

Previously visited pages and locally saved records may remain available. Weather and other operational feeds need an internet connection; this is not a complete offline EFB.

Reconnect when possible to refresh your briefing, then reopen the app if something looks stale.
