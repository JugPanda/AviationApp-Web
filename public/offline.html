Offline mode

AVWeather is available in limited offline mode.

The installed shell, tools, and recently cached screens can still open, but live aviation data like METARs, ADS-B flights, TFRs, and NOTAMs need a connection before they refresh.

Reconnect when possible to refresh your briefing, then reopen the app if something looks stale.

  • Cached app shell and key pages stay available
  • Last-fetched API responses may appear until you reconnect
  • Use current official sources before operational decisions
